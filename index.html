About

I'm a data scientist based in the Netherlands. My undergrad was in Human Ecology at College of the Atlantic in Maine, a liberal arts degree that combined a variety of disciplines, where my main focus has been environmental science and quantitative methods. After that, an MSc in Data Science at Maastricht University.

Moving between those fields meant getting used to translating across disciplines, working with people who have very different expertise, and thinking about data in terms of the real-world context it comes from. I currently lead the data team at StraTopo, building geospatial models for sustainable mobility. My technical background spans quantum ML, time-series forecasting, satellite image classification, and gene expression analysis.
